Private agreement package

Local prototype — not production-ready. This page encrypts plain text in your browser and does not upload, publish, or put agreement content on-chain. Use only a trusted, reviewed copy of this page. Hosting code can be changed to capture text or passphrases.

Before you begin

  1. The recipient generates a new random passphrase here and shares it with the sender through a separate secure channel. Use the generated value rather than a memorable phrase; 32 characters alone does not ensure randomness. Never send it in the package, email it with the package, or put it on-chain.
  2. The sender enters that same passphrase and encrypts the agreement. Download the encrypted package and deliver it separately.
  3. The recipient opens the package here and enters the passphrase to decrypt locally.

Keep a separate secure backup of the passphrase. Anyone with both package and passphrase can read the agreement; a lost passphrase cannot be recovered. The passphrase does not verify identities or authenticate who sent a package.

Privacy and legal limits

The package reveals its format, cryptographic parameters, and ciphertext length. Do not include names or other sensitive metadata in filenames or delivery messages. A published ciphertext or public hash may be retained and copied; hashes of known or guessable documents can reveal which document they represent.

This prototype has no identity verification, recipient public-key infrastructure, secure key exchange or recovery, audit, independent security review, or legal review. Encryption is not consent, signing, or proof of legal enforceability. Do not use the wallet controls or public contract hash for confidential agreement content.

Only plain text is supported. The page keeps working data in browser memory; clearing fields cannot guarantee secure memory erasure. Close the page when finished and handle downloaded plaintext carefully.
